# Review of 8 October 2026

An independent, read-only review of the project (by a second model, on request), condensed.
It ran the unit tests and linters, counted train/test proximity over the dataset file names,
checked seek accuracy on 12 field labels, cross-checked the field-label propagation against
neighbouring hand labels, and simulated the shot watcher. Anything else is from reading code
and docs; inferences are marked. Items done since are ticked.

## Main findings

1. **The field "ground truth" is weaker than the roadmap assumes.**
   - Carrying a hand label to a neighbouring hand label of the same video: in one of three
     testable pairs, a propagation that passed the 3 px drift check was 8–23 px off the other
     hand label (Truck Stop v Revolver 32194 → 31985). The other two agreed to 1–5 px.
   - Two hand labels 395 frames apart (San Francisco v Colorado 73883, 74278) agree to 2–5 px
     at the clicked far corners but differ by about 40 px at the 90th percentile elsewhere in
     the frame. The near part of most labels is extrapolation.
   - The drift check compares two runs of the same optical-flow estimator, so their errors are
     correlated. 593 propagated frames are about 62 views. The propagation does not mask
     players as the app does.
   - `scripts/benchmark_field_registration.py`: reference and "known focal" estimate share the
     focal length, so a focal error cancels; the summary is a median over frames that gave an
     estimate. Of 18 frames, 2 were silently wrong and 3 missing. The table still covers 18
     labels of 63. "10 games" is fewer venues (three US Open 2026, two Nationals 2025;
     inference from file names).
2. **No disc conclusion is statistically supported, positive or negative.**
   - Every variant was trained once, with the same seed (`train_model_subprocess.py` passes
     none).
   - 44–108 discs per test set: recall 0.49 on 98 discs is ±0.10. The decisive gap for "no
     mosaic" is 8 discs.
   - `best.pt` is chosen on the validation split, and the tables score validation and test
     together; the winner of seven was then picked on the same images.
   - Recall at a fixed confidence is not comparable between models (calibration).
   - IoU ≥ 0.5 on an 11 px box: a 2 px offset decides a hit. Possession needs the centre.
   - No held-out game. In `combined_discs_v4`, 29 of 53 old test frames have a training frame
     of the same game within 5 s; of the 55 own test frames, 3 within 30 frames, 17 within 300.
   - `label_files.split_of` hashes 300-frame groups independently, so neighbouring frames can
     land in different splits. Frames labelled from clips count as another video than their
     game.
   - Frames labelled from model suggestions favour models that resemble the suggester.
3. **Field registration trusts its wildest estimates most.** An estimate more than 8 yd from
   where the field was followed to replaces it outright (`field_registration.py`,
   `_evened_out`). The focal length is taken as known after 15 samples from about a second of
   one view; the main tab does not use the focal length a video's labels give. Likely main
   silent failure (inference): the mask cuts off the far end zone and the far goal line is
   taken for the back line.
4. **Failures are converted into plausible-looking output.** A detector exception returned no
   detections (shown as a close-up), a ByteTrack exception switched to per-frame IDs, a failed
   engine load ran three times slower, all without a word in the UI. ✔ shown on the frame now
   (`processing/health.py`).
5. **Environment and licence.** `YOLO_AUTOINSTALL=false` was set in three scripts only ✔;
   `requirements.txt` pins nothing ✔ (`requirements-lock.txt`); three OpenCV wheels
   (`opencv-python 4.12`, `-contrib 4.11`, `-headless 4.11`) share one folder; PolyForm
   Noncommercial on code that imports AGPL-3.0 Ultralytics and GPL-3.0 PyQt5 is probably not a
   licence that can be offered for the combined program (not legal advice). Suggested:
   AGPL-3.0.

## Other bugs and fragile spots

- Shot watcher: after a confirmed pause one frame with six players resumed the analysis ✔.
- Tracking and camera motion run across a cut for the 15 confirming frames before the reset.
- Jersey numbers are discarded at every reset, so at every close-up of an edited game.
- The swap and ID counts on the 21 clips come from a script that is not in the repository;
  the clips average 6 s and cannot show re-identification over 1.5–30 s. Possession has no
  accuracy measurement.
- Engine crash: cause unknown. See "Engines are loaded with the video" in MEASUREMENTS.md
  for what has been ruled out.
- `main.py` did not enable `faulthandler` ✔.
- Two tabs take the model lock blocking on the GUI thread (`easyocr_tab.py`,
  `homography_tab.py`): the window freezes while the main tab loads models.
- Frame-count settings (shot 15, possession 10, segmentation every 5, split groups of 300)
  mean twice the time on the one 29.97 fps video (`raleigh_vs_portland_2024`).
- No tests for the dataset builders, the worker, or the follower's jump and focal logic; no CI
  ✔ (`.github/workflows/checks.yml`).
- Confirmed good: seeking is frame-accurate on all 12 labels tried.

## Limitations

- Every component is tuned and scored on the same handful of games; tracking and possession
  have no ground truth.
- An 11 px, often blurred disc is near the limit of single-frame detection; the detector sees
  one frame although camera motion is known.
- Kit colour separates teams, not teammates; beyond 1.5 s only jersey numbers restore an
  identity, and few crops show one. IDs will keep climbing without identity in field
  coordinates and a roster per game.
- Module-level state in `processing/`: one pipeline per process, tabs can disturb each other.
- The app writes no tracks, possession or positions to disk.
- One developer, 25,800 lines; four readers and two trackers kept alive; `.git` is 521 MB,
  largely screenshots.

## Recommended order

**First week**
1. Safety day: engine workaround ✔, `faulthandler` ✔, auto-install off ✔, lock file ✔, failures
   shown ✔, shot-watcher fix ✔, CI ✔, half a day of crash bisecting ✔ (no cause found), single
   OpenCV wheel, licence decision.
2. Field benchmark v2 ✔ (partly: per game, not yet per venue or at held-out near points):
   all 63 labels, scored at clicked points and held-out near points,
   leave-one-venue-out, coverage, silent-failure rate (estimate given and over 2 yd), 90th
   percentile. Use within-reach label pairs to check the propagation.
3. Plausibility gate for the field ✔ (catches the far-off estimates only; see
   MEASUREMENTS.md): players' feet inside the field, agreement of the projected
   field with the mask, camera continuity, two agreeing estimates before a jump. Focal length
   from labels where the video has them.
4. Start labelling a disc test set: two or three held-out games, frames uniform in time and
   at least 10 s apart, suggestions hidden, "no disc" frames included, at least 500 discs.

**First month**
5. Retrain the field segmentation from masks rendered out of the field labels (near and far
   end zone as separate classes), keeping the camera fit. Label for breadth: 4–5 frames in
   each of 20–30 new games, each with a near point.
6. Disc: freeze the test set; match by centre within one disc diameter; recall at a fixed
   false-positive rate per frame; bootstrap intervals; three seeds for finalists. Then a
   detector with temporal context (three camera-stabilised frames).
7. Tracking ground truth: four clips of 45–60 s, every 30th frame, identities assigned by
   hand on the detector's boxes; IDF1 and IDs per true player. Then re-identification in field
   coordinates and a roster per game.
8. Export of tracks, numbers, holder and field positions.

**Later**: the learned field model if step 5 plateaus; state moved into objects owned by the
pipeline as modules are touched; possession accuracy; dependency replacement only if
distribution becomes real.

**Not worth time now**: more single-frame disc variants before the test set exists;
appearance re-identification; pipeline speed; refinement against painted lines; the genetic
calibration search if field registration replaces it; keeping DeepSORT, EasyOCR-only,
Florence, `yolo_digits` and RT-DETR working; chasing the engine crash beyond the half day.
